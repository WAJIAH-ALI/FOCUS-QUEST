# 🧠 FocusQuest

### Turn Productivity into an Adventure.

An AI-powered productivity companion designed to help people with ADHD stay focused, organized, and motivated through intelligent task management, gamification, and personalized guidance.

> Less pressure. More progress.
> [ FocusQuest Banner ]
> ## 📖 About

FocusQuest reimagines productivity by transforming everyday tasks into engaging quests. Built with accessibility and ADHD-friendly design principles, the app combines artificial intelligence, behavioral science, and gamification to reduce cognitive overload while encouraging sustainable productivity habits.

Rather than overwhelming users with endless task lists, FocusQuest provides an adaptive experience centered around a friendly AI companion named Pip, who helps users break down tasks, stay motivated, and celebrate progress.
